Sign-in settings

Let people sign in with their company account. Local accounts keep working, so you can always sign in as a local Super Admin if the directory is down. People from OpenID Connect and SAML use their identity provider's MFA instead of this app's two-factor codes, so require MFA there. LDAP only checks a password, so LDAP users follow this app's two-factor rules.
