Users

People can also sign in with LDAP / Active Directory, OpenID Connect (Entra ID, Google, Okta, Authentik) or SAML: set that up on Sign-in settings. Their access then comes from their groups at every sign-in.

  • Read-only: opens projects, maps and config backups. Passwords, keys and SNMP communities are hidden.
  • Configurator: builds configs, saves and deletes projects and maps, runs the converter, sees secrets.
  • Admin: also manages a client's people, SSH and EVE-NG settings, approves Lab work and fetches configs over SSH.
  • Super Admin: everything, in every client, plus users and clients. Must use two-factor sign-in.
